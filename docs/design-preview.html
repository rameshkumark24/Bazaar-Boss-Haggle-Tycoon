Bazaar Boss: two directions for the haggle screen

Both screens show the same moment in the core loop: Selvam, a tough bargainer, has dropped to his last price for a brass oil lamp, and it is your turn. They share the three adjectives below and the platform rules (Roboto, Material Symbols, one filled button). They differ in everything else. Pick one, or say what to change.

  • Talkative. The bazaar talks (PRD §1), so the customer's face and line get the most room.
  • Fair. "Fair, readable and never a gamble" (PRD §1, DR6 to DR10), so prices are the biggest numbers on screen, every number is labelled, and mood is a face plus a word.
  • Unhurried. No clock in a haggle (DR12) and no fake urgency (CR5), so nothing flashes, counts down or pulses.

What it changes

  • Density: airy. The top half is a scene with nothing to tap except the item. Controls live in one raised panel at the bottom.
  • Colour: a dark ink-blue night, warm paper and teak. Marigold appears only on the Offer button.
  • Type: the customer speaks in a poster face with Indian roots (Rozha One also covers Devanagari). The UI is plain Roboto.
  • Surfaces: depth from one soft shadow (the tag and the panel). Radii 8 and 16, so the panel's corners stay concentric with its buttons.
  • Motion moment: the face reacts to each offer. Brows and mouth move in about 250 ms.

What it changes

  • Density: tighter. Three full-width blocks (who, the price, your move) fill the screen edge to edge.
  • Colour: bright and flat. Turmeric and ink are fields, not decoration. Enamel blue appears only on the Offer button.
