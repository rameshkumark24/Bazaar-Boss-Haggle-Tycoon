A haggling game set in an Indian bazaar. Bid at kabadi auctions, spot the fakes, and bargain face to face with customers who hide their price but show their mood. Grow a footpath stall into a shop.

The promise

  • It never makes you watch an ad to keep playing. If there's ever an ad, it's one you chose to tap. Skip it and you lose nothing.
  • No gold, no energy bars, no pay-or-wait walls. You get ahead by bargaining well.
  • Fair you can see. After every deal, find out what they'd really have taken.
  • Plays offline. No sign-up, no account.
